8. KNOWN LIMITATIONS & PROTOTYPE BOUNDARIES
================================================================================

1. Date Evaluation Scope (Demo Date vs. Target Shift Date):
   - Current Prototype Behaviour:
     Credential validity and remaining days are evaluated against the fixed system demo date
     (2026-10-18, DEMO_DATE), not the future target shift date (e.g. 2026-10-20).
   - Operational Implication:
     If a staff member's certificate expires on 2026-10-19 and a manager assigns them to a shift
     on 2026-10-20, the prototype evaluates the credential as valid (+1 day remaining as of the
     demo date 2026-10-18) and permits assignment. On the day the shift is actually worked,
     the credential would be expired!
   - Production Requirements:
     In an enterprise production implementation:
     a) Primary Shift-Date Anchoring: The validation engine MUST evaluate credential validity
        relative to the shift date (shift.date) and shift end timestamp, ensuring the certificate
        remains valid throughout the entire duration of duty.
     b) Policy Grace Periods: System policies must define whether a grace period is permissible
        (e.g., 0 days for acute/emergency roles vs. 15-day provisional renewal window for outpatient clinics).
     c) Advance Expiration Alerts: When scheduling shifts weeks in advance, the system should flag
        credentials expiring between the booking date and the shift date.
     d) Automated Scheduled Audits: A daily background sweep must revalidate upcoming assigned
        shifts and automatically notify managers or revoke assignments if credentials lapse before the shift date.

2. Client-Side State Persistence:
   - All state modifications (simulated credential renewals, new shift assignments, and audit events)
     reside in browser memory. Reloading the page resets data to initial mock records. A dedicated
     "Reset Demo Records" button provides controlled manual resets.

3. Fictional Data & No External Issuer Verification:
   - Prototype does not connect to external certifying authorities (American Heart Association,
     State Nursing Councils) or hospital HRIS/payroll APIs. All credential attributes are simulated.

4. Role Credential Policy Scope:
   - Mandatory rules are fully configured only for the "Nurse" role (BLS + State Nursing Council).
     Physicians and pharmacists trigger an unconfigured role diagnostic rather than inventing rules.

5. Audit Trail Implementation:
   - The Validation Activity log provides educational and demonstrative audit records. It is not
     an immutable, tamper-evident regulatory compliance database.
